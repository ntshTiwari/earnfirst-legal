Earnfirst

Privacy Notice

Version 2026-11-draft · Last updated 20 November 2026 (analytics details)

Earnfirst is an iPhone app that limits the apps you choose and lets you earn more time on them by finishing short lessons toward your own goals. This notice explains what information the app uses, why, who helps us process it, and the choices you have.

1. Who we are

Earnfirst is provided by Nitesh Kumar, Bangalore, India (“we”, “us”). We decide how your personal data is used (we are the “controller”). Contact us at emailntsh@gmail.com about anything in this notice.

2. What stays on your iPhone

Earnfirst uses Apple’s Screen Time framework. Your Screen Time data never leaves your iPhone. That includes which apps you chose to limit, how long you use them, and the screen time numbers the app shows you. Apple keeps them on your device, and we can’t see them. Your daily pool of minutes is also kept on the device.

3. What we collect

  • Account. When you sign in with Apple or Google: an account ID from that service and, if they share it, your email address (with Apple this can be a private relay address) and name.
  • Goals and learning. The goals you write, the learning paths and lessons made for them, which lessons you open and finish, quiz answers, and any feedback you send about a lesson.
  • Settings and consent. Your daily allowance of free minutes, your analytics choice, and a record of when you accepted these terms and which version.
  • Subscription. Whether you have a free trial or subscription, which plan, and when it ends. Apple handles payment; we never see your card details.
  • Product analytics. How the app is used, for example screens viewed, lessons completed and buttons tapped. Each event comes with your device model, operating system and app version, language, region and time zone, an anonymous device identifier, and an approximate location (country or city) worked out from your IP address. These events never include your goal text, lesson content, the apps you limit, your screen time numbers or your name. You can turn analytics off in Settings › Privacy.

4. Why we use it

  • To run your account and the app, including writing lessons for your goals (performance of our contract with you).
  • To manage your subscription (contract).
  • To understand and improve the app through analytics (our legitimate interests, or your consent where the law requires it; you can turn analytics off at any time).
  • To keep records of consent and to meet legal obligations (legal obligation).

We don’t sell your personal data, and we don’t use it for advertising.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your account data while your account exists. When you delete your account, your account, goals, lessons and progress are removed from our database straight away; copies in routine backups expire within 30 days. Consent records may be kept as long as the law requires.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account any time in Settings › Delete account. This does not cancel an App Store subscription: cancel it in your iPhone’s Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions.
  • Turn analytics off in Settings › Privacy.
  • Screen Time access can be turned off in iOS Settings at any time; the app then stops limiting apps.
  • Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, California law or India’s DPDP Act), you can ask for a copy of your data, its correction or deletion, object to or restrict processing, and withdraw consent. Write to emailntsh@gmail.com. You can also complain to your data protection authority.

8. Age

Earnfirst is for adults aged 18 and over. We don’t knowingly collect data from anyone younger.

9. Security

Data is encrypted in transit, and access to it is limited to what running the app needs. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data.

10. Changes

If we change this notice in a way that matters, we’ll tell you in the app before it applies.
